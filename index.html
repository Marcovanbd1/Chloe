The person

Not a career politician.
A Stroud person.

Chloe Turner has lived in Minchinhampton for years. Her three children go to school here. She keeps bees, writes historical fiction, and knows the Cotswold commons the way you can only know a place you've actually walked, worked, and stayed in.

By training she is a chartered accountant. By vocation she is a councillor who shows up: at full council, at the town meeting about the lido, at the Showmen's Guild annual lunch, at the recording studio for Five Valley Sounds, the talking newspaper for blind and partially-sighted residents. She reads aloud the council leader's column so that everyone can hear it, not just those who can see.

She is also Leader of Stroud District Council, one of the most successful Green administrations in England, and a County Councillor for Minchinhampton, re-elected with a doubled Green group in 2025.

The fight right now

The Lido.
89 years of Stroud summers.

Stratford Park Lido opened in 1937. It has one of only four surviving inter-war concrete diving platforms in England. It is Grade II listed. And this summer it cannot open, because the infrastructure beneath the water has been ageing for 89 years and now needs replacing — to the tune of £5 million.

The council cannot find that money alone. But it is not walking away.

"A costed plan is being drawn up to do only those works needed to get the pool back open this season, and if we can get it open safely and affordably by the school holidays, we will do." — Chloe Turner, April 2026

The record

What Green leadership
has actually done

  • 276 affordable homes built through the council's own programme. Built, not promised., with a further 16 new homes secured in Kingswood and more in the pipeline in Horsley and Cam.

  • Full district-wide garden waste collection introduced, including rural and remote areas that previously had no service. Plus doorstep battery and vape recycling, toothpaste tube recycling, plastic film collections coming.

  • Carbon Neutral 2030 goal enshrined in the Council Plan, with a £100,000 Community Climate Change Fund reopened this year, funded by the Green budget amendment to the county budget.

  • The Missing Mile of the Stroudwater Canal is being restored: water is now flowing through newly rebuilt stretches between John Robinson Lock and Dock Lock for the first time since the M5 was built in the 1960s.

  • £494,000 raised for community projects through the Stroud Funding crowdfunding programme, supporting 33 local projects with council match funding.

  • Green group on GCC more than doubled in the 2025 county elections, from 4 to 9 county councillors, giving Stroud a stronger voice at the county level than ever before.

  • UBI Lab co-founded in Stroud, following a council motion on Universal Basic Income, one of the first of its kind in local government.

What is at stake

This may be one of the last elections
for the council you know

The Government is abolishing district councils. Stroud District Council, Green-led, community-rooted, 40 years in the making, will become part of a new unitary authority by 2028. This election matters because the values, the record, and the direction need to be locked in before the clock runs out.

Local Government Reorganisation

Gloucestershire is being restructured. Chloe is fighting for a single unitary rather than an East/West split that would cost local taxpayers an extra £9 million and break up established teams in social care, highways, and health.

Planning Under Pressure

Stroud's Local Plan was found unsound in 2025. Without it, developers can push hard. The Greens are protecting green field sites and delivering affordable homes, but they need strong representation at county to hold the line.

The Climate Is Not Waiting

The government redacted its own climate security report. Reform won seats across Gloucestershire. Chloe asks the difficult questions in committee, holds auditors to account, and refuses to let climate action become a footnote.

A County Council That Works

GCC faces a £60m funding cut over two years. Potholes, rural bus routes, youth services, public rights of way: Chloe has challenged every one of these. A larger Green group means a stronger, louder voice for Stroud at county.
